## What is imported

### MyCOL 2024/2025 (manually transcribed and reviewed)

MyCOL lists 66 critical occupations at the MASCO 2020 4-digit level. Nine were selected because they are relevant to this app's audiences and could be assessed against O*NET. Each record stores the 4-digit code, the Table 7 title and number, the 6-digit codes, the included job titles and the profile page. Where MyCOL names job titles, only those titles are classified as critical; the app says so and does not extend the classification to other titles in the same occupation.

### O*NET 31.0 (downloaded and regenerated by script)

For each mapped O*NET-SOC code: title, description and all 35 skill ratings on both scales, with the published value, N, standard error, confidence bounds, Recommend Suppress ("low precision indicator"), Not Relevant, date and domain source. Values are copied unchanged. Importance uses O*NET's 1–5 scale and Level its 0–7 scale. Neither is converted into the app's 1–5 self-ratings.

Regenerate with `pnpm --filter @workspace/api-server run import:onet` (set `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1` behind a proxy), or pass a directory of locally downloaded CSV files.

## How recommendations work

1. **Documented skills** are O*NET skills with Importance ≥ 3.0 on the 1–5 scale, excluding importance ratings flagged as low precision and level ratings flagged as not relevant. The 3.0 threshold is a SkillShift selection rule (the scale midpoint), not an O*NET classification.
2. Profile skills are compared with documented skill names using the existing `normalizeSkill` and the shared `skillOverlap` formula in `careerMatcher.ts` (the same formula as the career dataset matcher behind `/api/career/match` and the CV recommendations). No new scoring engine was added.
3. An occupation is recommended only when its mapping is verified, O*NET publishes ratings for it, and at least one documented skill is in the profile. The UI shows "N of M documented skills" and an explanation built from those facts.

These concepts are kept separate in the UI and API:

- **Personal readiness:** the existing illustrative proficiency coverage of the sample roles. Unchanged.
- **O*NET documented skill information:** United States reference data. It does not establish Malaysian demand.
- **MyCOL critical occupation status:** a TalentCorp classification, not a hiring probability.
- **DOSM statistics:** shown only on the Skills radar and not used in this ranking.
- **AI scenarios:** unchanged and unconnected.

## Limitations

- Matching is exact by name. Profile skills such as "Excel" or "Python" do not match O*NET's broader skill names (for example "Programming"), and no fuzzy matching or synonym guessing is applied.
- The roadmap continues to use the current sample target role. O*NET skills are not converted into roadmap proficiency targets.
- Only 9 of 66 MyCOL occupations are included. The other 57 are not represented, and their absence means nothing about their status.
