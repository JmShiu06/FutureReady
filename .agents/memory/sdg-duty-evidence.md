---
name: SDG duty evidence boundaries
description: Why occupational SDG links require duty-level evidence and stay separate from fictional AI explanations.
---

Use a specific UN target and documented duties together to support occupational thematic relevance; a technology, finance or skill label alone is insufficient. Partial crosswalks need explicit sub-role exclusions. Relevance is not measured impact or an official UN/MyCOL classification.

**Why:** The product requires defensible cited mappings without implying all critical occupations contribute to an SDG. Clinical nursing and HR labour relations have clearer evidence than generic digital or financial work.

**How to apply:** Preserve unassessed states when evidence is insufficient. Separate source publication/adoption dates from access/review dates. Do not fabricate skill requirements from duty citations.

Do not route real-occupation SDG tags through fictional-role, goal-only AI explanations without a separately approved contract change.

**Why:** A goal selector cannot identify the occupation or its reviewed sub-role scope; silently reusing it could misattribute fictional evidence to a real occupation.

**How to apply:** Keep reviewed occupational citations accessible without inference. Any future AI extension needs explicit context selection and must retain the synthetic-only profile boundary.
