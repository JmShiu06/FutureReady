# Reviewed pathway additions

Reviewed on 2026-10-23 against public Open University / OpenLearn pages.
These additions apply only to the personalised pathway catalogue, not occupation
requirements, proficiency equivalences, market scoring or the separate learning library.
Existing resource and role/SDG records are unchanged.

All three course descriptions explicitly label the course free. Recorded cost is
MYR 0 for learning content, not a converted or estimated price. No HRD Corp
claimability is asserted. Account, participation statement, device/internet and
qualification caveats remain as in the existing OpenLearn records.

## Learning how to learn

- [Provider description and outcomes](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/education-development/learning-how-learn/content-section-0):
  apply course ideas and activities to existing learning experiences and learn reflectively.
- [Preparation](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/education-development/learning-how-learn/content-section-4.1):
  analyse learning objectives, what must be learned and the assessment task, then plan.
- [Reflection and learning](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/education-development/learning-how-learn/content-section-9.1):
  prepare, explore, implement and review; check thoughts and actions while working,
  review both content and how it was learned, and use that review for the next task.
- Provider's “About this free course” metadata (also indexed on
  [the course page](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/education-development/learning-how-learn)):
  **6 hours study**, **Level 1: Introductory** → catalogue **Beginner**.
- Exact reviewed skill labels: **Active Learning**, **Learning Strategies**.
  Coverage is limited to applying and reflecting on new learning in a study/assessment
  context; it does not establish occupational capability or proficiency.

## Understanding management: I'm managing thank you!

- [Provider description and outcomes](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/leadership-management/understanding-management-im-managing-thank-you/content-section-0):
  self-management, including monitoring performance and applying ideas to one's work.
- [Section 4: Managing the time available](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/leadership-management/understanding-management-im-managing-thank-you/content-section-4):
  log activities, evaluate efficiency/effectiveness against criteria, identify and
  implement changes, then review outcomes against those criteria.
- Provider's “About this free course” metadata (also indexed on
  [section 3.2](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/leadership-management/understanding-management-im-managing-thank-you/content-section-3.2)):
  **5 hours study**, **Level 1: Introductory** → catalogue **Beginner**.
- Exact reviewed skill label: **Monitoring**. Partial self-performance coverage
  only; not supervision of others or organisational system evaluation. No extra
  skill aliases are added just because management topics are mentioned.

## Making decisions

- [Provider description and outcomes](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/leadership-management/making-decisions/content-section-0):
  more effective decisions, perspectives on effectiveness and decisions about risk.
- [Section 2.2, Activity 2](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/leadership-management/making-decisions/content-section-2.2):
  explicitly compares financial costs, opportunity costs, benefits and likely
  outcomes of options; distinguishes rational, psychological and social approaches.
- Provider's “About this free course” metadata (also indexed on
  [the end-of-course page](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/money-business/leadership-management/making-decisions/content-section-9)):
  **4 hours study**, **Level 3: Advanced** → catalogue **Advanced**. A short
  duration is not evidence of beginner level.
- Exact reviewed skill label: **Judgment and Decision Making**. Partial coverage
  of comparing options and risk, not validated occupational judgement.

## Evidence limits

The provider's expandable metadata was omitted by the text-only page extractor;
hours and levels above were checked in the primary-provider page search index,
not derived from activity timings, reviews, ratings or third-party listings.
Full-course hours are distinct from the app's separately labelled four-hour
portfolio practice estimate. SDG tags on the additions retain the catalogue's
app-reviewed learning intentions (education/work skills), not provider endorsement
or measured impact.

If future provider evidence does not establish duration or cost, retain `null`.
Do not infer free access from a participation statement alone. Unpriced resources
must not be selected as zero-budget options; missing durations must not produce
a complete finish date. Regression tests exercise these boundaries as well as
exact skill selection, course reuse and plan-wide/free-only budget handling.
